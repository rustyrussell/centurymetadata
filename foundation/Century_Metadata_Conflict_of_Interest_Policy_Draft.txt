CONFLICT OF INTEREST POLICY

Century Metadata Stiftung

Version: 1.0
Effective Date: [Date of Board Adoption]

1. Purpose

The purpose of this Conflict of Interest Policy is to protect the integrity, independence, and reputation of Century Metadata Stiftung (the “Foundation”) by ensuring that decisions are made solely in the best interests of the Foundation and its purpose. This Policy establishes clear standards for identifying, disclosing, and managing actual or potential conflicts of interest.

2. Scope

This Policy applies to:
- All members of the Board of Trustees;
- Members of the Technical Advisory Council;
- Any person holding a position of significant influence or decision-making authority within the Foundation (including, where applicable, executive staff or contractors with material decision-making power).

3. Definitions

3.1 A Conflict of Interest exists when a person covered by this Policy has a personal, financial, professional, or other interest that could reasonably be expected to influence, or appear to influence, their judgment or actions in carrying out their duties to the Foundation.

3.2 Conflicts may be actual, potential, or perceived.

3.3 Examples of situations that may give rise to a conflict include, but are not limited to:
   - Having a financial interest in a company, project, or individual that does business with, or seeks to do business with, the Foundation;
   - Serving on the board or in a decision-making capacity of another organization whose interests may compete with or be affected by the Foundation;
   - Having a close personal or family relationship with a person or entity that stands to benefit from a decision of the Foundation;
   - Receiving gifts, hospitality, or other benefits from parties seeking to influence Foundation decisions.

4. Disclosure Obligations

4.1 Annual Disclosure
All persons covered by this Policy shall complete and submit a written Declaration of Interests at the time of their appointment and annually thereafter. The declaration shall list all known actual or potential conflicts.

4.2 Ongoing Disclosure
In addition to the annual declaration, any person covered by this Policy must promptly disclose to the Board (or to the Chairperson) any new actual, potential, or perceived conflict of interest as soon as they become aware of it.

4.3 Content of Disclosure
Disclosures shall include sufficient detail to allow the Board to assess the nature and significance of the conflict.

5. Procedure for Managing Conflicts

5.1 When a conflict (actual, potential, or perceived) is disclosed or identified, the Board shall determine the appropriate course of action. The person with the conflict shall not participate in this determination.

5.2 Possible measures include, but are not limited to:
   - Full recusal from discussion and voting on the matter giving rise to the conflict;
   - Partial recusal (e.g., participation in discussion but no vote);
   - Public disclosure of the conflict in relevant Foundation records or reports;
   - In serious cases, resignation from the conflicting position or from the Foundation body.

5.3 In cases where a conflict cannot be adequately managed through recusal, the Board may decide that the individual should not participate in related decisions or activities.

5.4 All decisions regarding the management of a conflict shall be recorded in the minutes of the relevant meeting.

6. Record Keeping and Transparency

6.1 The Foundation shall maintain a register of disclosed conflicts of interest. This register shall be available for inspection by Board members and, upon request, by the Technical Advisory Council.

6.2 A summary of material conflicts and how they were managed may be included in the Foundation’s annual public report, to the extent consistent with privacy and security considerations.

7. Consequences of Non-Compliance

Failure to disclose a conflict of interest, or failure to comply with the procedures set out in this Policy, may result in disciplinary action by the Board. In serious cases, this may include removal from the Board or Technical Advisory Council in accordance with the Foundation Regulations.

8. Related Documents

This Policy should be read in conjunction with:
- Deed of Foundation
- Foundation Regulations
- Treasury Policy

9. Amendment

This Conflict of Interest Policy may be amended by a qualified majority decision of the Board of Trustees.

10. Final Provisions

This Policy enters into force upon adoption by the Board and remains in effect until amended.
